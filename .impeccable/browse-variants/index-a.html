BasketWise · Browse page — Option A refined

Three executions of the chosen direction.

The Ledger Grid (Option A) is the confirmed direction. All 3 pages now share a permanent category sidebar and identical filter/sort behavior — only density and how much weight savings gets differ.

Latest round of changes, in all three:
1. Categories are no longer a dropdown — a permanent sidebar, always visible, each category expanding into its subcategories accordion-style on click (matches your reference).
2. Sort adds "Savings: Low to High" alongside the existing options (7 total: Name A–Z/Z–A, Price low–high/high–low, Savings high–low/low–high).
3. Coles-only / Woolworths-only still means "this store is cheaper," not just "in stock here."
4. A3 is a new concept this round: sorted by biggest saving first by default, with the saving itself as a ribbon on the card image instead of a line of text below the price.
